# MIT-BIH source data

The 44 records in this package are unchanged publisher-verified files from the
MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, version 1.0.0, hosted by PhysioNet:
https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/

Database citation: Moody GB, Mark RG. The impact of the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia
Database. IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Magazine 20(3):45–50 (2001).
PhysioNet citation: Goldberger AL et al. PhysioBank, PhysioToolkit, and PhysioNet:
Components of a New Research Resource for Complex Physiologic Signals.
Circulation 101(23):e215–e220 (2000).

Source data license: Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0 (ODC-By 1.0):
https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/view-license/1.0.0/
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

The publisher SHA256 manifest is included as SHA256SUMS.txt. Annotation mapping,
selected lead, missing-file audit, boundary exclusions and per-record counts are
recorded in reports/dataset_manifest.json and reports/dataset_preparation.json.
This package contains the conventional 44-record subset, excluding the four
paced records 102, 104, 107 and 217. It does not contain synthetic ECG training
signals. The original archive is separately preserved under backups/.
